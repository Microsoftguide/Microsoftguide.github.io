Before you sign in

Confirm that you are on the genuine Microsoft sign-in page, check the account address you intend to use and make sure your device has a stable internet connection. Avoid sign-in links from unexpected messages.

Common login checks

  • Check for typing errors and keyboard layout differences.
  • Confirm whether the account uses a password, passkey or another sign-in method.
  • Check that the device date and time are correct.
  • Use a recovery method already associated with the account if verification is requested.
Never share your password or one-time security code. This website does not collect account credentials.
