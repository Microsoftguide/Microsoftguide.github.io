Prepare before seeking assistance

Write down the product name, device and operating system, exact error message, approximate time and steps already attempted. Keep passwords, one-time codes and payment details private.

Use official Microsoft resources

Navigate to Microsoft's official website directly for account-specific support, billing questions or product entitlements. Be cautious of unsolicited calls, pop-ups and unexpected remote-access requests.
